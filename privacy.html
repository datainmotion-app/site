Privacy Policy

Data in Motion is used only by its owner, with the owner's own accounts.

It collects no personal data from anyone else: no visitors, no viewers, no other users. The only data it handles are the authorization tokens of the owner's own accounts, which stay on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them.

The videos it produces use public datasets, credited in each video. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the settings of each platform.
